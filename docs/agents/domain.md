## Consumer Rules

- **Truth & Evidence**: All brand strategy and consulting recommendations must trace back to explicit user answers (`FACT`), user choices (`DECISION`), or validated calculations (`CALCULATION`). Hallucinated assumptions are prohibited.
- **Language**: All end-user interactions, UI questions, and client deliverables are strictly Persian-first, natural, respectful, and adapted to Iranian guild realities.
- **Sequential Phase Gates**: Progression across Phases 1 through 8 is strictly gated by `phaseGateValidator.js`. Phase skipping is prohibited.
