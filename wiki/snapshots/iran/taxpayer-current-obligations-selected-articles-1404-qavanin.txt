Current Article 2 — verified decision-useful meaning
- Retailers and guild/business units that deal directly with final consumers must, in addition to membership in the Taxpayer System, use a sales terminal.
- Taxpayers that do not deal directly with final consumers are not required by Article 2 to use a sales terminal, but they must issue their invoices through the Taxpayer System in the manner prescribed by the Tax Organization.
- Article locator: current consolidated Article 2, including the 1404-04-08 amendment.

Current Article 10 — verified decision-useful meaning
- Commercial persons must declare to the Tax Organization the account(s) used as commercial accounts and the unique identifiers of POS devices or electronic payment gateways used for their business activity.
- Article locator: current consolidated Article 10, amended 1404-04-08.

Current Article 15 — provenance note
