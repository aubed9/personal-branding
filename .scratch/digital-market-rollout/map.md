# Map: digital-market-rollout

## Destination

Produce the complete **Production Staging, Live AI Governance & Commercial Client Delivery Specification** for DIGITAL MARKET.
Reaching the end of this map looks like:
1. Security and proxy strategy for live Gemini API credentials in browser deployments is fully decided.
2. Deliverable presentation and client-ready export formats (interactive HTML report, standalone single-file master document) are standardized and prototyped.
3. Natural-language monetary amount extraction patterns (ریال، تومان، میلیون، میلیارد) are researched and defined for Persian freeform financial input.
4. Guild taxonomy mapping is audited and validated against national trade union classifications.
5. All open fog is either graduated into resolved decisions or consciously ruled out of scope.
6. Handoff to `/to-spec` with zero unresolved architectural ambiguities.
