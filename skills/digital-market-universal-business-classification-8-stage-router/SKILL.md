## 2. Why This Skill Exists & Architectural Mission

Every business operates within a unique operational, financial, regulatory, and commercial reality:
- A local car wash or oil-change shop lives on physical bay utilization, local map rankings, technician trust, and cash velocity.
- A third-wave coffee roastery balances sensory atmosphere, seat turnover, and retail bean sales.
- A B2B cloud accounting SaaS depends on recurring subscriptions (MRR), Samaneh Moadian compliance, and churn reduction.
- A heavy industrial toolmaker depends on machine tonnage, ISO tolerances under 3 microns, and enterprise procurement committees.

Traditional platforms reduce businesses to a single flat label, producing generic questions and disconnected advice. This skill centralizes the universal classification engine, routing 753 business types across 15 independent axes, executing open-ended diagnostic probing, composing chained adaptive questions, extracting facts from freeform Persian text, and ensuring zero corporate jargon leakage into traditional trades.

---

## 3. The Single-Label Fallacy vs. Multi-Dimensional Reality

A founder who states:
> «من یک آنلاین‌شاپ پوشاک هستم که قصد راه‌اندازی شوروم حضوری دارم و فعلاً خودم کارها را مدیریت می‌کنم»

cannot be categorized by a single label. They represent:
1. **Channel Model:** Hybrid (Online-First expanding to Physical Showroom)
2. **Offer Type:** Physical Product (Apparel)
3. **Scale:** Solo / Micro
4. **Founder Role:** Founder-Led
5. **Customer Model:** B2C

The router decomposes every business into 15 orthogonal axes without forcing arbitrary single-choice pigeonholes.

## 7. Open-Ended Diagnostic Probing (`step0_diagnostic_probing`)

Immediately after Step 1 (Stage & Description identification), the system executes an open-ended diagnostic probe:

> **«حیطه کاری شما دقیقاً چیست، خودتان کسب‌وکارتان را چطور می‌بینید و چه هویت، تمایز و ارزشی برای آن قائلید؟»**

### Objectives of Diagnostic Probing:
1. **Uncover Founder Mental Model:** Identifies whether the founder sees themselves as a neighborhood craftsperson, a high-growth tech innovator, an industrial linchpin, or an elite advisor.
2. **Anchor Strategy:** The extracted response is stored as immutable fact `FACT-P1-FOUNDER-VISION` and grounds all subsequent messaging, elevator hooks, and archetypes.
3. **Prune Redundant Steps:** If the founder explains their customer base, pricing model, or bottleneck in this probe, subsequent phase questions skip those already-answered topics.

## 8. Dynamic Chained Question Composition Formula

Static questionnaires are prohibited. All questions for Phases 1 through 8 are composed dynamically using the mathematical formula:

$$\text{ActiveQuestions} = Q_{\text{base}} + Q_{\text{industry}} + \sum Q_{\text{overlays}} - Q_{\text{irrelevant}} - Q_{\text{answered}}$$

Where:
- $Q_{\text{base}}$: Universal stage diagnostic questions (max 2–3 questions).
- $Q_{\text{industry}}$: Tailored questions specific to the exact trade (`BT-xxxx`).
- $\sum Q_{\text{overlays}}$: Questions injected by customer model, channel, maturity, and founder role overlays.
- $Q_{\text{irrelevant}}$: Strict subtraction of inappropriate questions (e.g. zero SaaS churn questions for car washes; zero foot traffic questions for machine shops).
- $Q_{\text{answered}}$: Subtraction of any question whose answer was already established in prior freeform text or diagnostic probes.

## 10. Unknown-Aware Hypothesis Registry

When a user responds with phrases indicating lack of data:
- *Explicit Ignorance:* «نمی‌دانم»، «اطلاعی ندارم»، «بلد نیستم»
- *Unmeasured Timing:* «هنوز اندازه نگرفته‌ام»، «حساب نکردیم»، «داده‌ای ندارم»، «ثبت نشده»
- *Uncertainty:* «مطمئن نیستم»، «شک دارم»، «تخمینی ندارم»
- *External Dependency:* «باید از حسابدار بپرسم»، «باید با شریکم هماهنگ کنم»

### Zero Fake Numbers Invariant:
The system **MUST NEVER** force fake or arbitrary numbers (e.g., inventing 35% margin or 50 daily visits).
Instead, it:
1. Registers the variable with status `OFFICIAL_UNKNOWN` in the Hypothesis Registry.
2. Formulates an actionable validation experiment in Layer 3 of the deliverable.
3. Annotates formulas in Layer 4 with `[فرضیه نیازمند تست - مجهول رسمی]`.
4. In Layer 5 (Exit Gate), adds explicit conditions to verify the hypothesis before permanent scale.

## 15. Operational Integrity, Guardrails & Veto Rules

1. **Strict Minimal Clarification Rule:** Ask only for information that changes downstream routing.
2. **Re-Entry & State Preservation:** When an earlier phase is modified, downstream phases update affected sections while preserving untouched decisions.
3. **Zero Hallucination / Zero Drift Invariant:** Recommendations must trace back to recorded facts, user choices, or 165-source knowledge nodes.
4. **Zero Corporate Jargon Leakage:** Any corporate jargon emitted to local trades (`IND-01`, `IND-05`, `IND-28`) is an immediate quality audit failure.
5. **Machine-Readable Output:** Every classification produces both a Persian executive summary and a structured YAML/JSON routing profile.
