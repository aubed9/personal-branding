# DIGITAL MARKET — Phase 5: Verbal Identity & Messaging System

## 1. Input Prerequisites
- Locked Phase 4 `Brand Identity & Character Foundation`.

## 2. Core Verbal Modules
1. **Brand Voice & 4-Quadrant Tone Matrix:**
   - Voice is constant (Who we are).
   - Tone adapts to situation (Delivering bad news, celebrating, teaching, selling).
2. **The 30-Second Executive Elevator Pitch (Hook Formula):**
   - Hook (Pain) -> Solution -> Proof -> Call to Action.
3. **Core Message Architecture & 3 Strategic Pillars:**
   - Pillar 1: Superior capability / Product truth.
   - Pillar 2: Customer empowerment / Emotional benefit.
   - Pillar 3: Long-term impact / Institutional trust.
4. **Brand Story & Narrative (Donald Miller StoryBrand 7-Part Framework):**
   - The customer is the Hero; the Brand is the Guide with a Plan.
5. **Lexicon & Vocabulary Rules:**
   - Words we celebrate and own.
   - Words and corporate clichés we ban forever.
6. **Persian Linguistic & Copywriting Guidelines:**
   - Punctuation, rhythm, cadence, formal vs conversational boundaries.

## 3. Phase 5 Gate Verification
- [x] Elevator pitch, one-liner, and 3 message pillars written and validated.
- [x] Tone matrix calibrated across positive, neutral, and crisis scenarios.
- [x] Brand vocabulary and banned words listed.
- [x] Formal `Verbal Identity & Messaging System` (سیستم هویت کلامی و پیام‌رسانی) generated.
