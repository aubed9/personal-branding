# DIGITAL MARKET — Phase 1: Business Foundation & Discovery

## 1. Input Prerequisites
- Activated `digital-market-business-context-router` profile.
- Initial founder intake statement or business concept.

## 2. Core Operational Modules
1. **Business Reality Snapshot:** Nature of operations, physical vs cloud assets, current monthly run-rate.
2. **Economic Engine & Unit Economics:** Revenue streams, gross margin per transaction/unit, break-even threshold.
3. **Operational Capacity & Bottlenecks:** Throughput limits (e.g. max cars washed per day, max SaaS concurrency, max factory tonnage).
4. **Hard Constraints & Boundaries:** Budget limits, regulatory licenses, technical limitations, founder time availability.
5. **Founder Ambition & 365-Day Target:** Quantifiable financial and strategic milestones.
6. **Separation of Knowledge Types:**
   - [FACT]: Proven, verified business realities.
   - [DECISION]: Consciously locked business choices.
   - [ASSUMPTION]: Unvalidated beliefs requiring research in Phase 2.
   - [UNKNOWN]: Explicitly acknowledged blind spots.

## 3. Dynamic Questioning Branching
- If **Micro Local Service**: Query location visibility, daily customer capacity, average ticket size, technician dependability.
- If **Hospitality/Food**: Query seat count, table turnover rate, raw ingredient margin, peak vs off-peak hours.
- If **B2B SaaS**: Query monthly recurring revenue (MRR), user acquisition channels, server infrastructure, onboarding friction.
- If **Industrial Manufacturing**: Query production line tooling, raw material supply reliability, delivery lead times, factory floor certifications.

## 4. Phase 1 Gate Verification
- [x] Business model and revenue logic clear and documented.
- [x] Operational constraints and unit economics recorded.
- [x] Critical assumptions isolated for Phase 2 research.
- [x] Formal `Business Foundation Profile` (شناسنامه بنیاد کسب‌وکار) generated.
- [x] Handed off to Phase 2 via `Research Brief`.
