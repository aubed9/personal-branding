# DIGITAL MARKET — Phase 4: Brand Identity Strategy & Character

## 1. Input Prerequisites
- Locked Phase 3 `Brand Strategy Foundation`.

## 2. Core Persona Dimensions
1. **Jungian Archetype Architecture:**
   - Primary Archetype (70% core identity: e.g. Ruler, Sage, Creator, Hero, Caregiver, Magician, Everyday).
   - Secondary Archetype (30% nuanced flavor: adds depth and human tension).
2. **Relational Role with Customer:**
   - Mentor/Guide (Yoda/Gandalf), Trusted Peer, Vanguard/Hero, Caretaker, Disruptor.
3. **Brand Personality Spectrum (Aaker 5-Dimension Framework):**
   - Sincerity, Excitement, Competence, Sophistication, Ruggedness.
4. **Behavioral Principles & Golden Rules:**
   - How the brand behaves under pressure, during complaints, and in everyday service delivery.
5. **Emotional Posture & Guardrails:**
   - "همیشه این‌گونه حس می‌شود:" (Must feel like)
   - "هرگز این‌گونه حس نمی‌شود:" (Must NEVER feel like)

## 3. Phase 4 Gate Verification
- [x] Primary and secondary archetypes selected and justified by strategy.
- [x] Relational role with the customer explicitly codified.
- [x] Behavioral principles and emotional boundaries locked.
- [x] Formal `Brand Identity & Character Foundation` (شناسنامه هویت و شخصیت برند) generated.
- [x] Handed off to Phase 5 via `Verbal Identity Brief`.
