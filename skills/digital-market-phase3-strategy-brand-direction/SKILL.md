# DIGITAL MARKET — Phase 3: Strategy & Brand Direction

## 1. Input Prerequisites
- Locked Phase 1 `Business Foundation Profile`.
- Locked Phase 2 `Market & Customer Intelligence Report`.

## 2. Core Strategic Formulations
1. **Target Segment Prioritization:**
   - Primary target (Highest lifetime value, lowest friction).
   - Secondary target (Expansion opportunity).
   - Deprioritized / Excluded audience (Anti-persona).
2. **The Only-ness Statement (Marty Neumeier Framework):**
   - *"کسب‌وکار ما تنها [رسته] در [محدوده] است که [تمایز بنیادین] را برای [مخاطب هدف] فراهم می‌کند تا [نتیجه تحول‌آفرین] محقق شود."*
3. **Value Proposition Canvas (Strategyzer / Osterwalder):**
   - Gain Creators & Pain Relievers aligned directly with Phase 2 JTBD.
4. **Category Frame & Competitive Anchor:**
   - Defining the frame of reference: What category are we leading, reshaping, or creating?
5. **Brand Promise & Proof Logic (Reason to Believe - RTB):**
   - The ironclad promise made to every customer and the tangible operational proof behind it.
6. **Strategic Boundaries (What the brand will NEVER do):**
   - Hard guardrails against commoditization, discount traps, or scope creep.

## 3. Phase 3 Gate Verification
- [x] Sharp, defensible Only-ness statement formulated.
- [x] Value proposition and customer problem cleanly matched.
- [x] Competitive positioning distinct from category incumbents.
- [x] Strategic boundaries locked.
- [x] Formal `Brand Strategy Foundation` (سند استراتژی بنیادین برند) generated.
- [x] Handed off to Phase 4 via `Identity Strategy Brief`.
