# DIGITAL MARKET — Phase 2: Research & Market Intelligence

## 1. Input Prerequisites
- Locked `Business Foundation Profile` from Phase 1.
- Specific `Research Brief` highlighting critical assumptions and unknowns.

## 2. Research Dimensions
1. **Target Audience & JTBD (Jobs-to-be-Done):**
   - Functional Jobs (What task are they hiring this business to do?).
   - Emotional & Social Jobs (How do they want to feel or be perceived?).
   - Pains & Anxieties (What stops them from buying?).
2. **Competitor & Alternative Matrix:**
   - Direct competitors (Same product/service).
   - Indirect alternatives (Doing it themselves, hiring a substitute, inertia).
   - Competitor weaknesses and blind spots.
3. **Pricing & Economic Elasticity:**
   - Reference pricing in the Iranian and local market.
   - Willingness to pay triggers and value perception anchors.
4. **Channel & Ecosystem Realities:**
   - Where the target audience actually spends time and makes buying decisions.
   - Regulatory and market barriers (e.g. Enamad, Tax authority, Guild permits).

## 3. Context-Specific Research Priorities
- **Local Service:** Local radius price check, customer review sentiment on Google Maps / Balad / Neshan.
- **Hospitality:** Local cafe crawl benchmarks, sensory and seating expectations, demographic spending habits.
- **SaaS:** Competitor feature checklists, pricing tier transparency, customer migration hesitation points.
- **Manufacturing:** Domestic industrial directory audits, supply chain lead-times, raw material import costs.

## 4. Phase 2 Gate Verification
- [x] Customer Jobs, Pains, and Gains validated with evidence.
- [x] Competitors mapped with clear vulnerability points.
- [x] Pricing benchmarks established.
- [x] Formal `Market & Customer Intelligence Report` (گزارش جامع هوش بازار و مخاطب) generated.
- [x] Handed off to Phase 3 via `Strategy Brief`.
