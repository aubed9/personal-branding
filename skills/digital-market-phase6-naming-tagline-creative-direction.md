# DIGITAL MARKET — Phase 6: Naming, Tagline & Creative Direction

## 1. Input Prerequisites
- Locked Phase 5 `Verbal Identity & Messaging System`.

## 2. Core Naming & Creative Modules
1. **Naming Criteria & Guardrails:**
   - Memorability, pronouncability, conceptual fit, global and local sensitivity.
2. **4 Naming Territories & Candidates:**
   - Territory A: Descriptive / Functional.
   - Territory B: Evocative / Metaphorical.
   - Territory C: Invented / Neologism.
   - Territory D: Heritage / Founder / Acronym.
3. **Phonetic, Domain & Legal Viability:**
   - Persian orthography and script flow.
   - Latin transliteration and global accessibility.
   - Domain extension check (.ir, .com) and social handle viability.
   - Preliminary trademark screening rules (Iran Trademark Registry context).
4. **Tagline & Slogan Architecture:**
   - Imperative, Descriptive, Superlative, Provocative, or No-Tagline strategy.
5. **Design-Ready Creative Direction:**
   - Visual metaphors, moodboard instructions, conceptual tension to resolve visually.

## 3. Phase 6 Gate Verification
- [x] Shortlisted names screened across phonetics, meaning, and trademark safety.
- [x] Winning name selected or explicit naming criteria locked.
- [x] Tagline finalized.
- [x] Formal `Naming, Tagline & Creative Direction Foundation` generated.
- [x] Handed off to Phase 7 via `Visual Identity Design Brief`.
